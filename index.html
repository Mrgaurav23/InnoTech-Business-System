Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a global IT services, consulting, and business solutions provider headquartered in Mumbai, India. A part of the Tata Group, TCS offers a range of services, including IT consulting, software development, and outsourcing, with a focus on digital transformation and innovation.

TCS CSBS Curriculam

The TCS (Tata Consultancy Services) CSBS (Computer Science and Business Systems) curriculum is a specialized undergraduate program developed by TCS in collaboration with academic institutions in India. It aims to bridge the gap between computer science and business, combining technical knowledge with a focus on understanding business operations, problem-solving, and decision-making.

Computer Science And
Business System (CSBS)

CSBS is an undergraduate program launched by TCS in 2018. It combines computer science with business knowledge, designed to equip students with technical skills and business insights, preparing them for the evolving needs of the technology and business sectors.

Vision

To Excel in the emerging areas of Computer Science and Engineering by imparting knowledge, relevant practices and inculcating human values to transform the students as potential resources to contribute innovatively through advanced computing in real time situations.

mission

The mission of CSBS is to integrate technology and business practices, providing students with the skills to design, implement, and manage technology-driven solutions in a business environment, preparing them for leadership roles in the evolving digital landscape
